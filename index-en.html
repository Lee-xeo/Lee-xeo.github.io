Shihao Li

Master’s student in artificial intelligence

I’m pursuing a master’s degree in artificial intelligence at Nanjing University’s School of Intelligence Science and Technology, where I work with my advisor, Professor Jiaheng Liu.

My earlier research focused on evaluating multimodal and omni-modal large models. I now focus on personal AI agents and AI education.

I earned my bachelor’s degree at Shanghai University’s School of Computer Engineering and Science (2021–2025).
