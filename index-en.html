Master's Candidate in AI at Nanjing University

Currently, I am pursuing a Master’s degree in Artificial Intelligence at the School of Intelligence Science and Technology, Nanjing University, under the supervision of Professor Liu Jiaheng. As one of the founders of the NJU-LINK Lab, my research focuses on pre-training, post-training, and evaluation of large-scale models (MLLM & AGI). I am dedicated to developing intelligent systems that benefit human society.
Earlier, I obtained my Bachelor's degree from the School of Computer Engineering and Science, Shanghai University (2021-2025).
